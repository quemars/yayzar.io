# Yayzar

Enterprise sales intelligence infrastructure for modern outbound teams.

Yayzar helps revenue organizations identify, enrich, prioritize, and operationalize high-value accounts through automated data intelligence and territory analysis workflows.

---

## Overview

Yayzar is a multi-page sales intelligence platform focused on account research, territory visibility, and outbound enablement.

The platform combines structured company intelligence, operational workflows, and scalable data infrastructure into a streamlined experience designed for agencies, sales teams, and growth operators.

---

## Core Capabilities

- Account intelligence and enrichment workflows
- Territory-based lead discovery
- Integration-ready platform architecture
- Security and compliance-focused infrastructure
- Responsive multi-page web experience
- High-performance static deployment architecture
