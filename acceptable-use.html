yayzar.com is presented as a responsible sales intelligence workspace, not a spam or scraping tool.

Responsible use

Users should respect consent, unsubscribe rules, CRM permissions, data accuracy, internal approval workflows, and applicable outreach regulations.

Prohibited use

Do not use account intelligence to misrepresent identity, bypass consent, send abusive campaigns, scrape restricted systems, enrich protected personal data unlawfully, or rely on unverified data as fact.

Data quality

Account research should be treated as a decision-support layer. Teams should verify company details, contact permissions, and source context before launching outreach.

Integration conduct

A production version should use scoped OAuth access, audit logs, revocable tokens, export controls, and admin approval for CRM or messaging-system connections.
