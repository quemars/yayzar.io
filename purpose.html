How it helps

A practical loop for finding and winning better accounts.

01

Search a market

Use location or ZIP code to find real nearby businesses and organize them by category, distance, and available public signals.

02

Rank the account

Prioritize records with useful sales context: website availability, local proximity, category fit, and enrichment needs.

03

Build the brief

Turn a business record into a short account summary, outreach angle, and next-step recommendation.

04

Move the workflow

Export the list, route the account, draft the opener, and track what happens next in the cockpit.
